# Shelf · Phase 2

*The concept step's phase 2, written on 2026-11-06. It rests on the idea alone.*

Phase 2 makes adding and finding books quicker, with the step's own proposals. It starts once phase 1 is done, and ends when a reader adds a book by scanning its barcode and finds any book by its title.

### P2.1 · Scan a book's barcode

Status: `planned` · Estimate: 1.5 dev-days · Depends on: M3 · Decisions: none, proposed

A reader adds a book by pointing the phone's camera at its ISBN barcode, instead of typing the number. Done when a scanned barcode adds the book, as a typed ISBN would.

### P2.2 · Find a book across shelves

Status: `planned` · Estimate: 1 dev-days · Depends on: M4 · Decisions: none, proposed

A reader searches all their shelves by title or author. Done when a search shows each matching book with its shelf, offline too.

### P2.3 · Sort a shelf

Status: `planned` · Estimate: 0.5 dev-days · Depends on: M4 · Decisions: none, proposed

A reader sorts a shelf by title, author or date added. Done when a shelf keeps the order the reader chose.
