# SHF-3 · Shelves the reader names: design

Agreed with the owner on 2026-11-12.

- A shelf is a name. The reader adds one from a field above the list; two shelves can't share a name, whatever the case.
- A book moves by a select beside it, which lists every shelf.
- "To read" stays the first shelf, and a new book goes there.
- The shelves live in memory with the books until STO-1 keeps them in a file.

Open: whether a shelf that holds books can be deleted, and what happens to its books then.
