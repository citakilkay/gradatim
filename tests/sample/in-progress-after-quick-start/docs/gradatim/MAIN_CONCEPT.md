# Shelf · The main concept

*The concept step's main concept, written on 2026-11-18. It rests on the idea alone.*

## The idea

A small web app where a reader keeps track of their books.

## What Shelf is

Shelf is for a reader who wants one small page that holds their books. They add a book by its title, put it on a shelf they name, and see at a glance what they have read this year. It runs on their own machine, with nothing to sign up for.

- **Small:** one page and a few routes, with no framework and no dependency.
- **The reader's own shelves:** the reader names the shelves and sorts their books their own way.

## What Shelf is not

- Not a catalogue or a social site: no accounts, no sharing, no lookup of a book's details yet.
