# Shelf · The roadmap

*The concept step's roadmap, written on 2026-11-06. It rests on D1, D2, D3, D4, D5, D6, D7 and D9 ([the decisions](decisions/README.md)). The estimates are senior dev-days, not dates.*

| Milestone | Estimate | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| The MVP | 9 dev-days | A reader, with the network off, can add a book by its ISBN, see its title, author and cover filled in once they are back online, and move it between their own shelves |
| Phase 1 | 5.5 dev-days | A reader sees how many books they finished this year, and a book added offline on one device shows on another once both are online |
| Phase 2 | 3 dev-days | A reader adds a book by scanning its barcode and finds any book by its title |

**The plan:** about 17.5 dev-days, 14.25 to 22.25; no dates.

## The MVP

1. M1 · Offline app shell
2. M2 · Shelves the reader names
3. M3 · Add a book by its ISBN
4. M10 · Add a book with the network off
5. M4 · Title and author from Open Library
6. M5 · Type the title and author by hand
7. M6 · Covers stored on the device
8. M7 · Move or remove a book
9. M8 · Edit the finished date
10. M9 · Ask Open Library about stored covers

## Phase 1

1. P1.1 · Books finished this year
2. P1.2 · Sign up and sign in
3. P1.3 · Shelves sync across devices

## Phase 2

1. P2.1 · Scan a book's barcode
2. P2.2 · Find a book across shelves
3. P2.3 · Sort a shelf
