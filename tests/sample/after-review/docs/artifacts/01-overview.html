Overview

Shelf

A small web app where a reader keeps track of their books on shelves they name, online and offline.

Made 2026-11-13 The plan about 17.5 dev-days Milestones 3

What it will be

A reader types a book's ISBN, and Shelf fills in its title, author and cover from Open Library. Books sit on shelves the reader names, the shelves work offline and sync across devices once the reader signs in, and the reader can see how many books they finished this year. Shelf is free and non-commercial, and has no second book source.

  • Books come from Open Library's ISBN API and Authors API, with no key (D1, D2).
  • Adding a book never blocks: it is saved at once, and the details fill in later or are typed by hand (D4).
  • Reading, To read and Finished to start with, renamable, and one shelf per book (D5).
  • Shelves and covers live on the device, and sync where the latest change wins (D3, D6, D9).
  • The count of books finished this year uses an editable finished date (D7).

Milestones

The MVP is Shelf offline on one device, phase 1 adds the yearly count and sync across devices, and phase 2 holds three proposals: about 17.5 dev-days in all.

  1. The MVP M1 to M10 9 dev-days An offline app shell, named shelves, adding by ISBN, online or offline, the lookup, typing by hand, stored covers, moving books, finished dates, and the question to Open Library.
  2. Phase 1 P1.1 to P1.3 5.5 dev-days The count of books finished this year, sign-in, and sync across devices.
  3. Phase 2 P2.1 to P2.3 3 dev-days Proposed: barcode scanning, search across shelves, and sorting a shelf.
