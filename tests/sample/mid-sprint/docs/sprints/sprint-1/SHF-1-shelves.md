## Goal

A reader names shelves and moves books between them, online and offline (D4).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A reader adds, renames and removes a shelf. **Shown 2026-11-05:** the shelves' tests.
- [x] A book moved to another shelf shows there at once. **Shown 2026-11-05:** the reader's check on the shelves page.
- [x] Moving a book works offline, and the move is kept in IndexedDB until the catch-up. **Shown 2026-11-05:** the test with the network off.
