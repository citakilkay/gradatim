## Goal

Each book has a page with its cover, its title and its authors, and a link back to Open Library (D3).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The book page shows the cover by its cover ID, and a blank card when there is none.
- [ ] Offline, the book page shows the title and the authors from the device's copy (D4).
- [ ] The page links to the book on Open Library.
