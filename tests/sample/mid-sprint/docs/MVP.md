# Shelf · The MVP

*The first release's stories, written by the concept step on 2026-11-04. A story keeps its id, also when it moves.*

### M1 · Add a book by its ISBN

Status: `planned`. A reader types or scans an ISBN, and Shelf fills in the title, the author and the cover from Open Library (D2, D3). Done when a book added by its ISBN shows all three.

### M2 · Shelves the reader names

Status: `planned`. A reader names shelves, such as Reading, To read and Finished, and moves books between them. Done when a book moved shows on its new shelf.

### M3 · Finished this year

Status: `planned`. The count of books moved to Finished since 1 January (D6). Done when the count matches the Finished shelf's books of this year.

### M4 · Sign in with an e-mail link

Status: `planned`. A reader signs in with a link sent by e-mail, and finds their shelves on any device (D5). Done when a second device shows the same shelves.

### M5 · Offline shelves

Status: `planned`. The shelves work offline, and catch up when the reader is back online (D4). Done when a book moved offline shows on the server once the reader is back online.
