Shelf

A small web app where a reader keeps track of their books, on shelves they name, online and offline.

Milestones

  1. The MVP, sprints 1 and 2, about 20 dev-days: a book by its ISBN, shelves, finished this year, sign-in, and offline shelves.
  2. Phase 1, from sprint 3, about 10 dev-days: a reading goal, a shared shelf, and notes on a book.
